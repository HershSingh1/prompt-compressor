Prompt Compressor

Select text (or click into a field) and press Alt+Shift+C or right-click → Compress prompt. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Mode

LLM mode sends your selected text to api.anthropic.com. Local mode never leaves the browser.

Local rule strength

Aggressive also drops hedges ("maybe", "I think", "if possible"), swaps "very big" for "huge", and shortens units and dates. It saves more but can shift nuance, so re-read the result. Stored in chrome.storage.local on this device only.
